Blended Program

Blended Courses & Video Tutorials

  • Challenge A new app left learners confused, and step-by-step in-person training was a drain on the team’s time.
  • Approach Built a video tutorial using screen recordings and AI voice-over, so learners could follow each step at their own pace.
  • Result Learners could learn the app on their own, which freed up team time for the hands-on, in-person part of the program.
Learner Resources

Face-to-Face amp; Learning Resources

  • Challenge Students struggled to retain anatomy lab content beyond the session.
  • Approach Designed supplemental study resources that chunk content to manage cognitive load, pair visuals with text (dual coding), and scaffold from guided to independent practice.
  • Result Students gained a flexible, self-paced way to review and reinforce lab learning between sessions.
